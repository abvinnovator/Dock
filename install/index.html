STEP 01

Pin Dock to Toolbar

Click the puzzle piece icon in Chrome's top-right toolbar and click the Pin icon next to Dock for instant 1-click access anytime.

Tip: Use shortcut Alt + Shift + D for instant open.
STEP 02

Dock Floating Tabs

Open the Dock popup and click ⊞ next to any tab. The page floats right inside your active window — stay in your current tab while referencing docs, notes, or videos.

Example: Pin canned responses while writing Gmail replies.

STEP 03

Instant Text Snippets

Save common replies or snippets under Snippets. Type do/name in any text field or editor — it auto-expands instantly without hitting Enter.

Example: Type do/meeting → auto-expands your Google Meet link.
STEP 04

1-Click AI Prompts

Highlight any text or right-click any image on any webpage -> click Dock -> choose ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini. Dock sends your content along with your custom instructions.

Example: Right-click article → "Ask Claude: Summarize in 3 bullets".
