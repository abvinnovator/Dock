Dock. Installed — here’s how to get going in a minute

Copy here. Land there. Never switch tabs.

Show Dock a field on another page once — a form, your notes, a CRM. After that, every bit of text you select ships straight into it with one right-click. Everything stays on this device.

STEP 01

Pin Dock to the toolbar

Click the puzzle-piece icon at the top right of Chrome, then the pin next to Dock, so it’s always one click away.

STEP 02

Show Dock the field, once

Select text → right-click → Dock → Ship to a field in another tab… Pick the tab, click the field and name it, say “SO ID”. Dock pastes it and brings you right back.

Spreadsheet? Choose Just collect, then paste everything as a column later.

Then it’s one right-click

STEP 04

Ask AI from anywhere

Select text or right-click an image, choose Dock, then ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini. Dock opens it in your own account, adds your saved instructions and sends it.

No extra subscription or API key — it uses the AI account you already have.
